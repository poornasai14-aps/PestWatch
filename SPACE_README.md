# PestWatch — Hyper-Local Crop Pest & Disease Early-Warning

Upload a crop photo → detects insect pests (YOLOv8) and leaf disease (36 classes),
pools reports on a map, finds outbreak clusters (DBSCAN), and warns nearby farms.
English + Telugu. Log in with the demo accounts: `officer/officer123` or
`farmer/farmer123`.
